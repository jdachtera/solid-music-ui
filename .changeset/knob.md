First release: `<Knob>`, from pocketrig's encoder knobs. Relative drag with Shift for fine control, steps with notches and named ends, double-tap reset, keyboard control, haptic and drag-gesture callbacks, and a hold that keeps a late value from snapping it back mid-drag. Skinnable through custom properties, `cap`/`indicator`/`valuePosition`/`radius`, a `face` for extra SVG, and `data-part` hooks.
